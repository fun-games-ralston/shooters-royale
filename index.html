RULES OF THE TRIAL

Everyone starts with 200 HP and one life. No respawns.
Last fighter standing wins the trial.
Headshots deal 2.5× damage. Legs deal 0.65×.
Health never regenerates on its own — eat to heal.
Enchanted golden apples grant gold absorption on top of 200 HP.
Coins: 60 per kill, +25 per headshot, 500 for a win, plus damage dealt.
Your first win each day pays double. Three daily challenges reset every morning.
Kills within five seconds of each other stack into DOUBLE KILL → TRIPLE KILL and pay a bonus.
Every weapon tracks its own mastery. Ten kills earns a bronze charm on the gun, 150 earns diamond.
On the leaderboard, your place is the hardest difficulty you have ever won on. Ties go to whoever has the most wins at that level. Playing Rookie a hundred times will not move you up — beating Veteran once moves you a whole band.
Bots fight each other too. Let them.
Use cover and the upper decks. Open ground kills.
Every weapon fires its own effect — rail beams, forked lightning, rockets, void blasts.
