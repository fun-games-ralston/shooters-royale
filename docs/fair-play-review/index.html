Plain-language finding

The game taught the wrong strategy.

If the board says “most wins,” and every setup counts, a smart player learns to finish the smallest match quickly. The player is responding to the system.

→
Fix the incentive: compare only standard Challenge matches. Leave the easy setup available in Custom.

Nightmare spikes

More players show Nightmare than Elite, which is unlikely to be a normal step-by-step learning curve.

Why it happens

Players could jump directly to Nightmare and lower the opponent count, so “difficulty” was not standardized.

Do not label ability from this chart. A one-opponent Nightmare win may be easier than a full Rookie lobby. Season 1 needs fixed rules before we can learn the real difficulty curve.
Proposed structure

One job for each mode

The game does not need a total redesign. It needs a clear boundary between competitive standing, free play, and friend battles.

The fair test

Seasonal Challenge

Everyone faces comparable conditions and climbs one visible ladder.

  • 7 opponents
  • 3 minutes
  • Win + at least 3 eliminations
  • Rookie → Nightmare
  • Armory loadout still matters
Counts for seasonal rank
The playground

Custom Battle

Keep the familiar choices, presets, and experiments players already enjoy.

  • 1 to 11 opponents
  • Any opponent skill
  • Any time and arena
  • Optional Hunted Mode
  • Coins, level, mastery stay
Never changes seasonal rank
The social match

Play with Friends

Use the same weapons, pets, maps, and look in an unranked two-player room.

  • Host-authoritative round
  • Shared PvE content
  • No solo rewards
  • No seasonal rank
  • Separate PvP score
Fun together, not ladder proof
Current feature-branch logic

How a tier advances

There is no hidden difficulty adjustment. One qualifying win moves the player one step. A miss repeats the same target.

STEP 01
Start at RookieA new season begins with no seasonal clear.
→
STEP 02
See briefingSix seconds of ladder position, target, and fixed rules.
→
STEP 03
Play the matchSeven opponents. Three minutes. Your Armory setup.
→
STEP 04
Check the clearDid you win and score at least three eliminations?
→
STEP 05
Record or retryAdvance one tier, or repeat the same target.
YES: tier cleared.
Record the best result, then move Rookie → Regular → Veteran → Elite → Nightmare.
NO: no tier clear.
Surviving with fewer than 3 eliminations is not enough. The player retries the same tier.

    What the data can and cannot say

    Where did the numbers come from?

    The game's public read-only leaderboard, recent-results, Preseason-board, and season-status functions. The snapshot was recorded on September 12, 2026 at 2:11 PM Pacific.

    Can we prove someone hacked the game?

    No. The aggregate data shows unusual, one-opponent-like play. The public feed does not include opponent count or a replay, so it cannot prove intent or developer-tools tampering.

    Do we know the correct Rookie difficulty?

    Not yet. There are no Season 1 results under the fixed 7-opponent format. Measure clears within 1, 3, and 5 attempts before changing the ladder.
